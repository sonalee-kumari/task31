Protecting the Planet

Founded in 2021, FreshWash combines high-tech fabric care with eco-conscious practices to bring you the freshest laundry experience in town.

🌿 Eco-First Commitment

Traditional dry cleaners rely on toxic PERC chemicals that damage fabrics and water tables. We exclusively utilize 100% biodegradable enzymatic detergents and purified ozone washing systems.

Our closed-loop water filtration recovers and recycles over 60% of rinse water, drastically cutting environmental footprints without compromising softness.

🔬 Precision Fabric Care

Every single garment undergoes multi-point inspection. We catalog fabric compositions, treat tough stains with customized temperature baths, and use gentle steam presses designed for silk, cashmere, and denim.

With individual batch processing, your garments never touch another customer's laundry, guaranteeing pristine hygiene every single wash.
